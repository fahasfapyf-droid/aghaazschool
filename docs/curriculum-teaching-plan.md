# Curriculum & Teaching Plan

## Domain model

- Curriculum: academic year + grade + subject scope.
- CurriculumTopic: ordered teaching unit with objectives and estimated periods.
- TeachingPlan: section-specific assignment of a topic to a teacher with planned dates and progress status.

## Statuses

Curriculum: DRAFT, PUBLISHED, ARCHIVED.

Teaching plan: PLANNED, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, SKIPPED.

## Operational flow

Academic Structure → Curriculum → Topics → Section Teaching Plan → Completion → Teacher Workspace / Monitor.

The timetable remains the schedule of teaching periods; the teaching plan is the expected curriculum progression.
